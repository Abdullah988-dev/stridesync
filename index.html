24/7 BACKGROUND TRACKING

Keep Pedometer Active in Background

Chinese OEMs (Realme, Xiaomi, Oppo, OnePlus) have aggressive battery savers. Follow these 2 steps so your steps never freeze when the screen is locked:

Realme / Oppo Setup Guide:

  1. Go to Settings > Apps > App management > StrideSync.
  2. Tap Battery usage and select "Allow background activity" & "Allow auto-launch".
  3. Open Recent Apps drawer, swipe down or tap the two dots on StrideSync, and choose "Lock" (🔒 icon appears).

Xiaomi / Poco / Redmi Setup Guide:

  1. Long press StrideSync app icon > tap App info (ⓘ).
  2. Enable "Autostart" toggle.
  3. Under Battery Saver, choose "No restrictions" (Default MIUI kills apps after 10 mins).
  4. Lock app in Recent Apps drawer.

Samsung Galaxy Setup Guide:

  1. Go to Settings > Battery and device care > Battery.
  2. Tap Background usage limits > Never sleeping apps.
  3. Tap + and add StrideSync to the list.

OnePlus Setup Guide:

  1. Go to Settings > Battery > More settings > Optimize battery use.
  2. Find StrideSync and set to "Don't optimize".
  3. Lock the app in the recent tasks overview.
